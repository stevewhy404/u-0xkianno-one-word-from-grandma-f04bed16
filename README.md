One word, and nothing else: the word grandma would text about AI.

**robit** — her typo for "robot". Warm, a little baffled, and exactly the
kind of word she would actually send her grandchild about the machines.
It lands as a joke with no caption and no context: one misspelled word,
and you can hear her voice in it.
